# 📊 Madhav Ecommerce Sales Dashboard

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** designed to analyze ecommerce sales performance, profitability, quantity sold, customer payment modes, categories, sub-categories, states, and monthly trends.

## 📌 Dashboard Preview

![Madhav Ecommerce Sales Dashboard](Screenshot\Full_Dashboard.jpeg)

## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to transform ecommerce sales data into an interactive dashboard that helps users quickly understand:

- Overall sales and profit performance
- Quantity sold across different states
- Category and sub-category performance
- Monthly profit trends
- Payment mode distribution
- Profit contribution by sub-category
- Quarterly performance

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Sales & Profit KPIs
The dashboard provides high-level KPIs for:
- Total Sales Amount
- Total Profit
- Total Quantity
- Average Order Value

### 2. Quantity by State
A state-wise comparison of quantity sold, including:
- Madhya Pradesh
- Maharashtra
- Uttar Pradesh
- Delhi

### 3. Quantity by Category
Category-wise quantity distribution:
- Clothing
- Electronics
- Furniture

### 4. Profit by Month
Monthly profit analysis helps identify profitable and loss-making months throughout the year.

### 5. Quantity by Payment Mode
The dashboard analyzes quantity based on different payment methods:
- COD
- UPI
- Debit Card
- Credit Card
- EMI

### 6. Profit by Sub-Category
Sub-category performance is visualized to compare profit contribution across products such as:
- Printers
- Bookcases
- Saree
- Accessories
- Tables

### 7. Interactive Filters
The dashboard includes interactive filters for:
- Quarter
- Overall/All selection

These filters allow users to explore the data dynamically.

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the dashboard:

- Clothing contributes the largest share of quantity among the displayed categories.
- COD represents the largest share of quantity among the displayed payment modes.
- Monthly profit varies significantly, with both profitable and loss-making months.
- Printers have the highest profit among the displayed sub-categories.
- Madhya Pradesh has the highest quantity among the displayed states.
